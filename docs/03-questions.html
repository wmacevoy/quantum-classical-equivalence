The plan

Three mysteries we will look behind

  1. Probability. Why does the wavefunction give only probabilities, $|\Psi(x,t)|^2$? In the textbook this is the Born postulate.
  2. Measurement. What happens when we look? Does the wave really "collapse"?
  3. Entanglement and causality. Alice and Bob share an entangled pair, light-years apart. Bob measures his particle. Does Alice's particle change instantly? Bell's theorem and its experiments rule out the obvious local explanations. Is something going faster than light?
Route: start from Newton, add a cloud, a Fourier transform, and one approximation, and arrive at Schrödinger. Then use that view on all three.

The third question is the one we're really after. Keep it in mind; we come back to it on slide 15.
