Why the cat?

Real, yet mysterious

  • To the cat the dot is real: it moves, it follows rules, it can be chased.
  • It is also mysterious: no mass, no smell, it jumps across the room instantly, and it can never be caught.
  • We are amused because we are in on the secret. The dot is the projection of something the cat cannot see: a hand, a pointer, a beam.
Today's claim: a lot of what makes quantum mechanics feel mysterious is the dot on the wall. Let's look at the hand.

The cat isn't stupid. Given what it can observe, its model of the dot is pretty good: the dot is real, it has dynamics, and it does strange things. What the cat lacks is the higher-dimensional picture that makes the strangeness ordinary.

Set the expectation: we won't replace quantum mechanics. We'll find a vantage point where quantum mechanics is a projection of something with classical-looking dynamics, and see which mysteries go away and which don't.
