What this view does not do

  • It doesn't explain spin, or the boson/fermion rule. Pauli exclusion can be represented, but not derived.
  • It doesn't explain the Standard Model. Fields, particle creation and annihilation, and the forces themselves are outside it.
  • It doesn't explain general relativity. Gravity and curved spacetime are not part of the picture.

Keep this short. The transform is about one-particle (and few-particle) non-relativistic dynamics: it shows what must be added to probabilistic classical mechanics to get Schrödinger's equation. It says nothing about where spin, the particle content of nature, or gravity come from.
