Ideas 2 and 3

Two tools from numerical methods

2Fourier transform in momentum

$$\hat w(x,\hat p) = \int dp\; e^{-i\hat p p/\hbar}\, w(x,p)$$

Derivatives become multiplication, and the reverse:

$$\partial_p \;\to\; \frac{i\hat p}{\hbar}, \qquad p \;\to\; i\hbar\,\partial_{\hat p}$$

$\hat p$ has units of length. This is where $\hbar$ enters: it is the conversion between momentum and its conjugate length.

3Centered difference

From a Taylor expansion of $V$ about $x$:

$$\varepsilon\,\frac{\partial V}{\partial x} \approx V\!\left(x+\tfrac{\varepsilon}{2}\right) - V\!\left(x-\tfrac{\varepsilon}{2}\right) \;-\; \frac{\varepsilon^3}{24}\,\frac{\partial^3 V}{\partial x^3}$$
  • This is the first thing taught in numerical differentiation.
  • Exact for linear and quadratic $V$, because $V''' = 0$.

Both tools are standard. The surprise is what happens when we combine them with idea 1.
