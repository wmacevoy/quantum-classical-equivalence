Mystery 1: probability

The Born rule is a shadow

$$|\Psi(x,t)|^2 = \int dp\; w(x,p,t)$$

The position probability is the momentum marginal of $w$: its shadow on the $x$ wall. Integrating over $p$ is the same as setting $\hat p = 0$ in $\hat w = \Psi^*(x)\,\Psi(x)$. No extra postulate is needed.

Harmonic oscillator ($\hbar=M=\omega=1$), where classical flow is exact: $w$ turns rigidly.
